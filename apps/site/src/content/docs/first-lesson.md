## Before you start

Sign in to [the workspace](/host/). A tutor and one student need:

- a Tutoring space;
- the student, with their language pair;
- a deck filed under the student;
- for Notes, homework and the student's own page, a plan that includes **Homework and Notes**.

Students, decks and live sessions are free. Notes, homework, access links, learner work and identified sessions are part of a paid plan. Without it, those places show a **Homework and Notes** lock with a **Plans** button, which opens billing. The plan belongs to the space owner. See [account and billing](/docs/account/).

## Create a Tutoring space

1. Open the space switcher and select **New space**.
2. Enter the **Space name**, choose **Tutoring**, and select **Create space**.

Your first space starts as Classroom. To change it, select **Choose experience** on its empty Library, or open **Space settings**, and choose **Tutoring**.

## Add the student

1. Under **Students** in the sidebar, select **Add student or group**.
2. Choose **Individual**, enter the student's **Name**, and select **Save**.

The student's place opens in the Library. Each student has their own language pair, and a new student has none yet. Select **Set language**, choose the **Students’ language** and then **Teaching**, and save. Word lookup needs this pair.

## Create the student's access link

1. On the student's place, find the **Access links** card below the decks and select **Create link**.
2. On **Create an access link**, keep the learner and expiry, and select **Create link**.
3. On **Copy this link now**, select **Copy link** and send the link to the student. The full link is shown only once.

The link opens the student's own page with their lesson outcomes, homework and feedback. See [access links](/docs/learner-links/).

## Prepare the deck

On the student's place, select **New deck**. The deck is filed under the student and opens in the editor. Add slides and questions, or select **Sample lessons** to copy a complete language lesson. Wait for **Saved**. See [edit and save a deck](/docs/deck-editor/).

## Teach the lesson

1. Select **Start session** in the editor or in the deck's details panel.
2. Share the join code or link with the student. They join on their phone or computer.
3. Move through the slides with **Next**. A question opens when its slide is current; use **Close answering** and **Reveal results** or **Call out the correct answer**.
4. Select **End session**, then **End session** in **End this session?**.

If you leave the console or reload the page, select **Rejoin** under **Live now** in the Library or the editor. See [run a live session](/docs/live-session/).

## Write the Notes

After **End session**, the session's Notes page opens. Its heading names the student and the deck.

1. Enter the **Outcomes**, one per line. The student sees them on their page.
2. Enter a **Next session** note. Tutors only: it is shown with the student in the Library and at the top of their next deck.
3. Add **Tutor notes**. They stay private.
4. Check the homework the deck carries, and its recipients.
5. Select **Save notes**.

If you leave before saving, the Library shows **Write the notes for {student}’s session, {deck}**. See [session Notes](/docs/notes/) and [homework](/docs/homework/).

## Review the homework

When the student has done the homework, open their place and select **Review learner work**. Open a response, write feedback, and select **Share feedback**; **Save draft** keeps it with you. The student sees shared feedback on their page. See [review learner work](/docs/review/).

For the next lesson, read the **Next session** note on the student's place and open the earlier Notes from the **Notes** group below the decks.
