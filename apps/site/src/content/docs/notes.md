## Open the session's Notes

After ending a session, continue to its Notes page. You can also return through the Library's prompt **Write the notes for {student}’s session, {deck}** (**Write the notes for {deck}** when the session has no student), or through the **Notes** group on the student's place in the Library, which lists each session's Notes by title.

Notes belong to the session that was taught. The heading names the student and the deck, for example **Session notes — Léa, Le train est parti**.

Session Notes and homework need a plan that includes **Homework and Notes**. Without it, the page shows a **Homework and Notes** lock with a **Plans** button in place of the form. The plan belongs to the space owner.

## Write and save

| Field | Content and audience |
| --- | --- |
| Outcomes (one per line) | Short statements of what was achieved. Shared through learner links when the session has a learner context. |
| Next session | A note for tutors only. For a student, it is shown with that student in the Library and at the top of their next deck. |
| Tutor notes / Private notes | Private teaching observations, available to authorized colleagues in the space. |
| Homework | Published tasks and their learner recipients for this session. |

Enter outcomes on separate lines. Keep **Next session** actionable. Review the homework inherited from the taught deck, adjust its instructions or recipients, then select **Save notes** or **Update notes**.

![Session Notes form with shared outcomes, the Next session note, tutor notes, and the homework section](/docs/images/session-notes.png)

*Outcomes are shared with learners; Next session and Tutor notes remain on the teaching side.*

Sessions without a learner context retain their Notes in the space. A context-linked session shares only its learner-facing fields and the tasks each learner is entitled to receive.

## Carry over notes from presenting

The live **Private notes** scratchpad is kept on the current device. After ending the session, its contents prefill the Notes form when appropriate. Save the form to store them with the session.

If saved Notes and separate local notes both exist, the page shows **Unsaved tutor notes** (**Unsaved private notes** for a session without a student), kept on this device from an edit that was not saved. Select **Add to private notes** to append that text to the field, then save, or **Download private notes** to keep a text copy.

## Use Notes as a presenter

A Presenter can read shared session Notes and keep **Notes on this device**. These local notes stay in that browser. Use **Download private notes** to export them. An Editor or Owner saves changes to the shared session record.

If a save is refused because your sign-in expired or your access changed, the form stops accepting edits and an alert says the notes were not saved. Your tutor notes are kept on this device. Select **Download notes** to save the outcomes, Next session and tutor notes as a text file before leaving. Sign in again, or ask a space owner to review your access if further shared editing is needed.

If the save fails because of the connection, the alert says **Notes were not saved. Check your connection and try again.** Your entries stay in the form.

## Handle concurrent changes

If another tutor changes the homework while your form is open, the save reports a conflict. Copy your edits before reloading, review the latest assignments, then reapply the intended changes. Existing learner responses keep the instructions they were submitted against.

Trashed sessions show saved Notes as read-only. Restore the session to resume normal editing.
